つづく

Tsuzuku: “to be continued”. Generations of Japanese viewers saw the word at the end of an episode. A century after a boy in a sailor suit bowed to the camera, anime is made in Tokyo and Seoul, financed from Riyadh and Los Angeles, and watched by hundreds of millions of people who will never visit Japan. What has not changed is what it does. It makes drawings move, and the people watching them are moved.
