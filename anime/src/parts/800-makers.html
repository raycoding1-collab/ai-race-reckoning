The makers

Twenty people who shaped it

Anime is made by hundreds of people per production, most of them uncredited outside Japan. These twenty, directors and also a colourist, an animator and writers, changed what the medium could do. Names are given in Japanese with the family name first.

手塚治虫

Osamu Tezuka

1928–1989

The “god of manga” who brought cinematic storytelling to comics, then built the TV anime industry with Astro Boy.

Astro Boy · Kimba · Phoenix

奥山玲子

Reiko Okuyama

1936–2007

Hired at Toei in 1957 as an in-betweener, she rose to become one of the first women to direct animation in Japan.

Hakujaden · Hols · Natsuzora's model

高畑勲

Isao Takahata

1935–2018

He studied French literature, never animated a frame himself, and directed some of the most daring films Ghibli made. In his spare time he translated Jacques Prévert's poems.

Heidi · Grave of the Fireflies · Kaguya

宮崎駿

Hayao Miyazaki

born 1941

His family's firm made rudders for Zero fighters during the war. His films love aircraft and distrust what they are for, and two of them have won Oscars.

Totoro · Spirited Away · The Boy and the Heron

出﨑統

Osamu Dezaki

1943–2011

When a scene peaked, Dezaki froze it and had the frame repainted as an illustration, the “harmony” cel. Split screens and repeated shots did the rest, and TV anime still speaks his grammar.

Tomorrow's Joe · The Rose of Versailles

富野由悠季

Yoshiyuki Tomino

born 1941

Turned the robot show into war literature, with teenage soldiers on both sides and no clean victories.

Mobile Suit Gundam · Char's Counterattack

松本零士

Leiji Matsumoto

1938–2023

His spaceships are old things: a battleship raised from the seabed, a steam train leaving the platform for the stars. Every voyage he wrote is also a farewell.

Space Battleship Yamato · Galaxy Express 999

金田伊功

Yoshinori Kanada

1952–2009

Animators still speak of a Kanada school. He drew explosions as hard-edged shapes and timed movement in sudden snaps, and a generation copied him frame by frame.

Galaxy Express 999 · Harmagedon

保田道世

Michiyo Yasuda

1939–2016

Ghibli's colour designer from the start until 2014. “Color has a meaning,” she said, “and it makes the film more easily understood.”

Colour design for nearly every Ghibli film

大友克洋

Katsuhiro Ōtomo

born 1954

His manga drew a ruined Tokyo down to the broken glass, with an engineer's patience. The film he made from it, Akira, showed the world what hand-drawn animation could do at full stretch.

Akira · Memories

押井守

Mamoru Oshii

born 1951

A basset hound wanders through almost every film he makes. Oshii lets his characters stop the plot to think aloud, and The Matrix borrowed from his Ghost in the Shell.

Angel's Egg · Patlabor 2 · Ghost in the Shell

庵野秀明

Hideaki Anno

born 1960

He started as the fan who animated the Daicon convention films, and spent a quarter of a century on a robot franchise about depression and, finally, recovery.

Gunbuster · Evangelion · Shin Godzilla

今敏

Satoshi Kon

1963–2010

He cut between dream and waking so smoothly that you notice only afterwards. Four features and a TV series in nine years; he died at 46 with a fifth film unfinished.

Perfect Blue · Millennium Actress · Paprika

幾原邦彦

Kunihiko Ikuhara

born 1964

He left Sailor Moon to make Utena, a fairy tale in which the prince is a girl, the princess is a prize won in sword duels, and the two of them break the story open together.

Sailor Moon · Revolutionary Girl Utena

渡辺信一郎

Shinichirō Watanabe

born 1965

Directs like a DJ, mixing genres and music. His shows taught the West that anime could be cool.

Cowboy Bebop · Samurai Champloo · Lazarus

湯浅政明

Masaaki Yuasa

born 1965

Yuasa lets a line wobble, stretch or look unfinished if the feeling needs it. In 2013 he co-founded Science SARU, the studio that later made Dandadan.

Mind Game · Ping Pong · Devilman Crybaby

細田守

Mamoru Hosoda

born 1967

He keeps returning to summer holidays, big family houses and the internet as a second world, with an original film roughly every three years since 2006.

Summer Wars · Wolf Children · Mirai

新海誠

Makoto Shinkai

born 1973

He made his first short alone on a Power Mac. Fourteen years later Your Name became one of the biggest films ever released in Japan.

Voices of a Distant Star · Your Name · Suzume

岡田麿里

Mari Okada

born 1976

She stopped going to school as a teenager and later wrote Anohana, about a boy who has done the same. One of anime's most prolific screenwriters, she began directing in 2018.

Anohana · Maquia

山田尚子

Naoko Yamada

born 1984

A director of glances and footsteps who finds drama in how people stand, wait and almost speak.

K-On! · A Silent Voice · The Colors Within

This is a list of people who made anime. Manga artists whose stories it adapted, such as Hiromu Arakawa, Hajime Isayama and Naoki Urasawa, appear in the reels beside the adaptations, Urasawa with Monster (2000–2010) and Pluto (2020–2026).
