Interlude · Anime and the world

Where it landed, and what it meant there

Anime did not spread evenly. It arrived through whatever door was open: a public broadcaster short of programmes, a toy company's ad deal, a dubbing studio in wartime Beirut, a pirate tape, a streaming service. And once it arrived, people made it their own. Choose a pin. The map is centred on Japan, as Japanese school atlases are.
