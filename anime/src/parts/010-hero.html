Drawnto Move

The oldest Japanese animation known to survive lasts three seconds. A boy in a sailor suit writes four characters, 活動写真, katsudō shashin, “moving pictures”. Then he turns to us, lifts his cap and bows. The second character, 動, means to move. It would go on to name the art form, and the feeling the art form gives.
