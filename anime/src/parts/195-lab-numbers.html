From a cottage industry to a ¥3.84 trillion business

In 1917 a Japanese cartoon was a few minutes of paper cut-outs. In 2024 the anime industry, counting broadcast, film, streaming, merchandise, music, games and live events, earned a record ¥3.84 trillion, about $25 billion, and most of it now comes from outside Japan.
