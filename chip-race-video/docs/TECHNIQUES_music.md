# Silicon Shield: music techniques (research, 2026-10-10)

Target: dark cinematic electropop + chiptune, 128-132 BPM (use 130), F minor, synthesized female lead.
Pipeline: Python + numpy/scipy/pyworld, CPU only (4 cores, 15 GB), no torch. Numbers are starting points; verify by ear and by spectrogram.
Tags: [TESTED] = run in this container; [REACH] = URL answered, not downloaded; [KNOWLEDGE] = standard practice, not re-verified online.

## 0. What exists today (skimmed)
- sing.py: Festival HTS (slt) -> WORLD (harvest/cheaptrick/d4c) retime/re-pitch; vibrato, scoop, formant warp, breath, detune, "gang" layers. 5 ms frames.
- music.py/dsp.py: polyBLEP saw/pulse, NES triangle, LFSR noise, supersaw, biquad, convolution reverb, ping-pong, compressor, limiter, vocoder, sidechain; master = hp28 -> comp 1.6:1 -> match_eq -> softclip -> limiter -1 dB at -11 LUFS.
- cinema.py: FM bell, brass, string hit, taiko, risers, tape stop, stutter. The kit is already broad; the gains are in vocal realism, arrangement contrast, and mix discipline (sections 1, 2, 4).

Recommended vocal upgrade path (all feasible offline-ish): Kokoro af_heart/af_bella speech per line -> WORLD analysis (existing) -> retime/re-pitch (existing) with the fixes below; MBROLA us1 only as a 16 kHz reference/second layer.

### WORLD improvements (sing.py, the highest-leverage section)
- Vibrato: do not apply from note start. Delay 0.25-0.4 s, fade in over 0.3 s, then rate 5.5-6.5 Hz (slightly accelerating +0.5 Hz over the note), extent +-30 cents on short notes up to +-60 cents on held belts, plus 1/f jitter (rate +-0.3 Hz random walk) and 0.5-2 cents of white micro-jitter. Only notes > 0.5 s get vibrato. Add a small amplitude tremolo locked in phase (~1.5 dB) and let formants breathe slightly with it. [KNOWLEDGE; rate 5-7 Hz, extent < 1 semitone: UCL/Sundberg handout]
- Pitch model: 4-6 Hz lowpassed overshoot at note transitions (overshoot ~15-25 cents, settle 60-80 ms), portamento 40-90 ms on same-vowel steps, scoop up from -50..-100 cents over 80-120 ms on phrase-starting accented notes, fall-off at phrase ends (-100..-300 cents over 150 ms with decaying breath). Pre-lower pitch ~1% on voiceless consonant neighbours (microprosody). Keep the autotune-flavour on hooks only: quantise to the F minor scale with 20-30 ms retune time for the "pop" chorus, 80-120 ms for verses.
- Formant handling on high notes: above ~C5 (523 Hz) f0 outruns F1, so singers tune F1 up to f0 ("formant tracking"). Implement: warp factor = clip(f0/(F1_ref*1.0), 1, 1.6) for open vowels; plus a singer's-formant boost of +4..+6 dB peak at 2.8-3.2 kHz Q~2 on belts (F3-F5 cluster per Sundberg; UCL handout). Do not shift the spectral envelope by the pitch ratio when transposing (WORLD keeps the envelope separate from f0, good); small formant raise 3-6% for a female timbre. Soften by limiting warp to 1.35 to avoid chipmunk.
- Consonants and timing: put onset consonants 60-120 ms before the beat (already done); keep consonant duration at natural length (never time-stretch unvoiced segments; stretch only vowels, using the stretched spectral frames). Add a 5 ms click-free fade on plosive bursts, +3 dB on /t k s/ to survive the mix, and clamp sibilants: compress 5-9 kHz (already present). Add glottal onset before vowel-initial words (4-8 ms creak: random jitter 3% on f0).
- Breathiness: scale aperiodicity (d4c) upward in the 2-8 kHz bands: ap_band = ap + b*(1-ap) with b = 0.1 (belt) .. 0.5 (intimate), and mix a separate band-passed (1-6 kHz) noise breath at -28 dB under phrase onsets/ends; insert inhales (60-200 ms, 0.5-4 kHz noise swell) before phrases (breaths() exists; shape them with attack 80 ms, decay 120 ms).
- Dynamics: map intensity to spectral tilt (+3..+8 dB tilt at 3-8 kHz belt vs -4 dB whisper) and to f0 jitter (less in belt). Use `pw.cheaptrick` q1 default -0.15; try q1=-0.05 for less over-smoothed (buzzier but brighter) envelopes. d4c threshold 0.85 default; use 0.3 for noisier/breathier.
- Double tracking: 2 doubles, each with independent timing offset N(0, 12 ms) per word (not constant), pitch offset N(0, 8 cents) with its own vibrato phase, formant +-2%, panned L 0.6 / R 0.6, level -9..-12 dB under the lead, high-passed at 200 Hz and shelved -3 dB above 8 kHz so they thicken without sizzle. Chorus gang: 4-6 layers at -14 dB, detune +-10..-20 cents, wide.
- Chain (extends vocal_bus): HPF 90-110 Hz (12 dB/oct) -> subtractive 250-350 Hz -2..-3 dB Q1 -> compressor 1 (4:1, attack 3 ms to catch peaks, release 60 ms, 3-4 dB GR) -> presence +2..+3 dB at 3-4 kHz Q0.8 -> air shelf +3 dB at 10 kHz -> de-ess 5-8 kHz -> compressor 2 (2:1, attack 20 ms, release 120 ms, 2 dB GR) -> saturation (tanh, drive 1.2, 10% parallel). Sends: plate 1.8-2.2 s decay, predelay 25-40 ms, HPF 300 Hz / LPF 6 kHz on the return, level -18 dB; quarter-note-dotted ping-pong -22 dB, filtered 300-4000 Hz; duck both sends by the lead (sidechain 6 dB) so words stay clear. Automate send levels: dry in verses, wet at ends of phrases and in the post-chorus.
- Modern pop gloss: formant-preserving +12 cents sharpening on the chorus lead is cheap, and a parallel octave-up layer (existing "oct") at -16 dB with +4 dB at 8 kHz gives the "synthetic diva" sheen.

## 2. Arrangement and composition (130 BPM, F minor)
Length: 3:15-3:30 = ~110 bars at 130 BPM (1 bar = 1.846 s). Suggested map (bars):
1. Intro 1-8: chiptune arp (16th, 25% pulse) + filtered pad + distant impacts; no drums for 4 bars, hats enter bar 5; rising HP filter.
2. Verse 1 9-24: bass (triangle/sub) + sparse kick on 1 & 3, clap on 2 & 4 only from bar 17; vocal intimate, dry; staccato string or chip stabs.
3. Pre-chorus 25-32: kick four-floor enters; snare roll 8ths (bar 29) -> 16ths (31) -> 32nds (32); noise riser from bar 29 (see section 3); filter sweep up; pitch-rise in bass; harmony climbs bVI -> bVII -> V (Db - Eb - C).
4. Drop gap: last 1/2 beat silence (or all but vocal tail + reverse cymbal) before the chorus: the biggest cheap impact.
5. Chorus 1 33-48: full kit, supersaw chords, reese + sub, chip lead hook, vocal doubled + octave, gang on the title line.
6. Post-chorus 49-56: instrumental "hook" (chip lead + vocal chops "Si-li-con") for 8 bars, drums thin out at bar 53 to prepare verse 2.
7. Verse 2 57-72: add one new element per 4 bars (counter-line, tom pulse) so energy doesn't reset; vocal slightly brighter.
8. Pre-chorus 2 73-80, Chorus 2 81-96: as before but add counter-melody and a half-time pad layer; extra crash/impact at downbeat.
9. Bridge/breakdown 97-104: strip to pad + piano/bell + vocal; half-time feel (kick on 1, snare on 3), tempo-synced long reverb; at bar 103 reverse riser + tape stop (exists) -> bar 105 silence.
10. Final chorus 105-120: up 2 semitones to G minor (safest for the vocal range; chorus loop Eb - Bb - F - Gm) or 3 semitones to Ab minor (more dramatic, check the top note stays under ~G5). Do it with a pivot: last pre-chorus bar ends on the new key's V (Eb for Abm: Eb7); add one more voice and an extra harmony (3rds/5ths) on the vocal, supersaw octave doubling, 8th-note rolling bass.
11. Outro 121-128: arp alone, drums fade, reverse swell, final stab at -12 dB.

Recipes:
- Chord loop (F minor, one chord per bar or 2 per bar): verse Fm - Db - Ab - Eb (i - VI - III - VII) with bass moving on roots, chorus Db - Ab - Eb - Fm (VI - III - VII - i), pre-chorus Db - Eb - C - C7 (bVI - bVII - V - V7 for harmonic-minor lift via A natural). Use Fm / Db / Bbm / C (i VI iv V) for the bridge. Avoid chord changes faster than 1 bar in verses.
- Hook: 4-8 note motif, mostly stepwise with one leap (a fifth or a sixth) at the start of the title; rhythm with syncopation (dotted 8th + 16th) landing on the "and of 2"; repeat identically 2x then answer a 4th higher. Place it in vocal, then chip lead (octave up, 25% pulse with 10 Hz vibrato fade-in), then bells in the outro: the same motif in 3 timbres is what makes it feel composed.
- Countermelody: a chip arpeggio or string line that moves when the vocal holds (call and response), contrary motion to the vocal; keep it in a different octave (C6-C7 for chip) and narrower stereo; mute it under the lead syllable (duck 3 dB).
- Tension/release toolkit: (1) 1/2-beat to 1-bar silences before downbeats, (2) high-pass the whole mix to 400-800 Hz over 2-4 bars then slam back, (3) snare roll accelerating (quarter -> 8th -> 16th -> 32nd) with rising pitch +12 semitones, (4) reverse reverb/crash ending on the downbeat, (5) pitch-dive sub drop (see 3), (6) remove kick for the last 2 bars of pre-chorus, (7) gated-reverb snare in breakdowns, (8) lift register of melody by 1 octave in final chorus.
- Dynamic contrast in LUFS-short-term: verse -16..-14 LUFS-S, pre-chorus building to -13, chorus -10.5..-9.5, breakdown -20. Chorus should be 4-6 dB louder than verse in perceived loudness; use arrangement not limiting to achieve it.
- Genre cues: minor-key electropop (The Weeknd / Grimes / Chvrches / Perturbator-ish cinematic) uses sparse verses with bass-pluck, 2-bar pre-chorus risers, and "wall" chorus synths with chip arps cutting through; chiptune should appear as accents (hook, arp, fills) not as the whole bed.

## 3. Sound design recipes (numpy)
- Kick (punchy): sine with exponential pitch drop f(t)=50 + 160*exp(-t/0.018) Hz (end ~45-55 Hz at F = 43.65 Hz root: tune to F1 = 43.65 or C2 = 65.4), amplitude env exp(-t/0.28) for 350 ms, add a 3 ms click (filtered noise burst, HP 2 kHz, -14 dB) plus a 1-2 kHz pitched "beater" tone decaying in 10 ms, soft-clip tanh(2.5x) then LP 6 kHz. Layer a sub-kick (40-55 Hz sine, 300 ms) at -3 dB. HPF 30 Hz. Phase-align so waveform starts at zero crossing going positive.
- Snare: body = two sines 185 and 330 Hz with pitch drop 12%, decay 120 ms; noise = white noise bandpassed 1.5-9 kHz, env exp(-t/0.11); add 3 ms transient click; layer with a clap and a 180 Hz tom. Gated reverb tail (decay 400 ms cut at 250 ms) on the chorus.
- Clap: 4 bursts of bandpassed noise (1.2 kHz, Q 1.5) spaced 9-13 ms with random spacing +-2 ms, each decay 10 ms, then a 100-150 ms tail (decay 70 ms); layer with snare; reverb send 12%. Slight stereo widen (Haas 8 ms on one copy, HPF 500 Hz).
- Hats: 6 detuned square waves at 205.3, 304.4, 369.6, 522.7, 540, 800 Hz (808 ratios), HP 7 kHz, closed decay 40 ms, open 250 ms, velocity swing and +-3 dB humanising; 16ths with accent on off-beats; swing 0-8%.
- Supersaw: 7 polyBLEP saws, detune offsets [-0.11, -0.063, -0.02, 0, 0.02, 0.063, 0.11] semitones*detune (about +-20 cents at center-heavy setting), center level 1.0, sides 0.7 mixed from a non-linear curve (Szabo, "How to Emulate the Super Saw"); random start phases; HP the stack at 150 Hz and keep the sub to mono bass; stereo by panning voices +-0.8 alternately; chorus 0.3 Hz depth 6 ms; filter LP 6-9 kHz with envelope; add a 15-20% sub-octave sine below. Chord stacks voiced in thirds/fifths: Fm = F3 Ab3 C4, doubled up an octave.
- Reese bass: 2-3 saws detuned +-7..+-15 cents at 43-87 Hz, slow phasing 0.3-0.7 Hz, LP 400-900 Hz with 2:1 resonance, distort tanh(3x), band-split: <100 Hz mono sine layer (clean), >100 Hz distorted layer; sidechain 70% depth. Lowest note F1 (43.65 Hz): check small speakers by adding the 2nd harmonic via saturation.
- FM bass/pluck: 2-op FM, ratio 1:1 or 1:2, modulation index envelope 4 -> 0.5 over 120 ms for a bite; carrier sine; add noise click. FM bell: ratio 3.5, index 3 decaying over 1.2 s (already in cinema.py).
- Plucks: saw -> LP with envelope 4 kHz -> 400 Hz in 150 ms, amp decay 250 ms, resonance Q 4; chord stab = 3 voices detuned +-8 cents, delay ping-pong at 3/16 note, reverb 1.5 s.
- Pads: 3-4 detuned saws (+-5..+-12 cents) + a triangle, LP 1.5-3 kHz, slow attack 0.4-1.2 s and release 1.5 s, chorus + phaser (0.2 Hz), sidechain 30-45% depth; high-pass 200 Hz. A formant/vowel pad ("aa/oo", via WORLD choir already in sing.py) adds the "cinematic" choir.
- Risers: (a) white noise with bandpass sweep 300 -> 12000 Hz exponential over 2-8 s, Q 2, amplitude rising 30 dB, optional tremolo accelerating 4 -> 20 Hz; (b) saw stack pitch rising +12 semitones, detuned, with LP opening; (c) reverse cymbal: reversed crash/noise tail; (d) snare-roll acceleration; (e) Shepard tone (3 octave-spaced layers, Gaussian amplitude window) for endless rise; end every riser 1/16 before the downbeat then 1/8 note silence.
- Impacts/downlifters: sub drop = sine 120 -> 30 Hz in 0.8 s plus LP noise burst + reversed reverb tail; a boom = sine at 45 Hz decay 1.4 s through convolution reverb (5 s); a stinger = brass hit (existing) + taiko + clang.
- Chiptune authenticity: NES 2A03: pulse duty 12.5/25/50/75% (25% and 75% sound identical), 16 volume levels (quantise envelopes to 4 bits), triangle = 32-step 4-bit stairs (no volume, tuned in 16 steps), noise via 15-bit LFSR with 16 period table, short mode 93 steps for metallic; DPCM kick low rez. Non-linear mixer: pulse mix = 95.88/(8128/(p1+p2)+100), tnd = 159.79/(1/(t/8227+n/12241+d/22638)+100). [nesdev wiki "APU mixer"; vgmrips.net/wiki/NES_APU]. Run at native rate, 1.79 MHz/16 pulse timer: freq = 1789773/(16*(t+1)); quantise pitch to that timer value (it detunes the upper notes slightly: authentic). Arp: fast arps at 60 Hz frame ticks (16.7 ms, or 130 BPM 16ths = 115 ms). Bitcrush the final chip layer 8 -> 5 bits only on fills; downsample 22 kHz; keep it dry (no reverb) or only gated reverb so it contrasts with the polished synths. Use 1 chip layer per frequency band (bass triangle, lead pulse 25%, arp pulse 12.5%).

## 4. Mixing and mastering by algorithm
- Gain staging: render each stem at -18 dBFS RMS average (-6 dBFS peaks max), master bus peak before limiter about -6 dBFS; use floats (never clip until the limiter). Track LUFS-S per bus to check balance.
- Target levels (RMS relative, with vocal at 0 dB): kick -6, snare/clap -8, bass -8, lead synth -10, pads -14, hats -16, arp -14, vocal doubles -10, FX -18. Vocal sits ~2-3 dB above the loudest synth in the 1-4 kHz range.
- EQ per element:
  - Kick: HP 30 Hz, +3 dB at 55 Hz, -3 dB at 300 Hz (mud), +2 dB at 3 kHz (click).
  - Sub/reese: LP 120 Hz for the sub layer (mono), reese HP 120 Hz and cut 250-400 Hz -2 dB; saturate harmonics at 2nd/3rd.
  - Snare/clap: HP 150 Hz, +3 dB 200 Hz body, +2 dB 5 kHz snap, -3 dB at 400 Hz.
  - Supersaw/pad: HP 150-200 Hz, -3 dB at 300 Hz, notch 1.5-3 kHz around the vocal (-2..-4 dB, Q 1) via dynamic ducking, shelf +2 dB 8 kHz.
  - Chip: HP 120 Hz, LP 12 kHz (aliasing tame), -2 dB 2.5 kHz when vocal is present.
  - Hats: HP 400 Hz, -2 dB 3 kHz, +2 dB 10 kHz.
  - Vocal: see section 1.
- Sidechain: kick-keyed on bass/pads/synths. Depth: bass 70-80% (full duck), pads 35-45%, leads 15-25%, reverb returns 30%. Shape: attack 1-3 ms (immediate), release 120-180 ms at 130 BPM (one 16th = 115 ms; set release so the volume has recovered by the next 8th, 230 ms), curve exponential (1 - depth*exp(-t/tau), tau = 0.06 s). Also a vocal-keyed 2-3 dB duck on pads/arp (50 ms attack, 120 ms release). Existing sidechain_curve is the right idea; deepen bass and add the vocal duck as a dynamic EQ (duck 1.5-4 kHz only).
- Parallel compression: drum bus: 8:1, attack 10 ms, release 80 ms, 10-15 dB GR, blended at 25-35% under the dry; vocal: heavy 10:1 with 12 dB GR, fast release, blended -10 dB. Glue bus: 2:1, attack 30 ms, release auto (150 ms), 1.5-2 dB GR.
- Saturation: tanh with drive 1.5-3 on bass bands (with oversampling 4x via resample_poly to prevent aliasing), tape-like asymmetric clipping on drums for even harmonics; apply on stems, not only master. Always band-split before saturating bass to keep the low end clean.
- Stereo rules: everything below 120 Hz mono (HP the side channel at 120-150 Hz with a 2nd-order filter: S = highpass(S)). Kick, bass, lead vocal, snare centered. Widen with Haas/short delays (7-17 ms) on pads/doubles/harmonies only above 300 Hz; supersaw pan spread 80%; hats/percussion pans +-0.3..0.6; reverb returns fully wide. Check mono compatibility: sum L+R, require < 3 dB loss in 100-5 kHz bands and correlation > 0.3. Use mid/side EQ: +1.5 dB 10 kHz on side for air.
- Reverb and delay: 3 buses: (1) room 0.6-1.0 s predelay 10 ms on drums/clap (12%), (2) plate/hall 1.8-2.8 s, predelay 25-40 ms, HP 300 Hz LP 6 kHz for vocal and synths (-18 dB), (3) long cathedral 5-6 s for transitions only. Delay: 3/16 note (346 ms at 130 BPM) ping-pong, feedback 35-45%, filtered 300-4000 Hz; BPM at 130: quarter = 461.5 ms, 8th = 230.8 ms. Duck returns with the dry signal's envelope (10 dB, 100 ms release) so effects bloom between phrases.
- Mastering chain (in order): linear-phase-ish gentle EQ (HP 25 Hz 12 dB/oct; -1.5 dB at 250 Hz Q 0.7; +1 dB at 3 kHz; +1.5 dB shelf 10 kHz) -> multiband compression (<150 Hz 2:1, 150-3 kHz 1.5:1, >3 kHz 1.5:1, ~1-2 dB GR each) or at least a bass-only band to keep the kick consistent -> bus glue compressor 1.5-2:1, attack 30 ms, release 200 ms, 1-2 dB GR -> tape/soft-clip saturation (tanh, drive 1.1-1.3, 4x oversampled) taking 1-2 dB off the peaks -> stereo widener on side >300 Hz (+1..+2 dB) -> lookahead true-peak limiter (5 ms lookahead, release 50-100 ms, ceiling -1.0 dBTP, 4x oversampled detection; gain reduction 3-5 dB) -> dither TPDF to 16 bit when exporting WAV.
- Loudness: Spotify normalises to -14 LUFS integrated, true peak -1 dBTP (and has a -11 "Loud" option); Apple Music -16; YouTube about -14. Electronic/pop is commonly mastered -11..-9 LUFS (hotter, a creative choice) (sources: roexaudio, masteringbox, soundbridge; verify against platform docs). For the video, target -12 LUFS integrated, true peak <= -1 dBTP, loudness range LRA 6-9 LU, short-term loudness ranging -17 (verse) to -10 (chorus). The current -11 LUFS is fine; avoid pushing the limiter beyond 5 dB GR (kick becomes soft).
- Measurement tools in this env: scipy + pyloudnorm (pip works) for ITU-R BS.1770 LUFS and true peak (use 4x upsample), pedalboard 0.9.26 wheel [TESTED download] for Limiter/Compressor/Reverb/Convolution/PitchShift at C++ speed (a drop-in replacement for the slow Python compress/limiter loops), librosa for spectra. Reference matching: compare 1/3-octave spectra to a reference track and correct within +-3 dB (match_eq does this at strength 0.6).
