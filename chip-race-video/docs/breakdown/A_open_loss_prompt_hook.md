## 1.1 Concept in one line
A luminous TikZ construction sheet on which the spark is literally the plotter pen. Every drawing event is the sung word or beat made into geometry, and a running TikZ listing top-left lights up line by line as each primitive is plotted (the joke: the model "draws a unicorn in TikZ", from ellipse / rectangles / beziers). The whole 9.3 s is ONE continuous 2D-world camera (world units are TikZ units, y up) with ~23 keyframes, plus one hard cut.

## 1.3 Rendering technique
- **Bg**: one fullscreen fragment shader (`BG_FRAG`): analytic graph paper in world space through the rolled camera (minor/major lines with pixel-size-based density fade), orange wavefront reveal, pen-warmth glow, fade-to-black. No textures.
- **Drawing**: one `LineBatch(200000, blend 'add')` (GPU capsule segments in 2D px). Every stroke is data: `Stroke{pts, lengths, t0, t1, ease, kind, width, dash, group}` plus a precomputed `tD[i]` (time the head reaches each point, from inverting the ease). One draw function renders any stroke at time t with: head position `k = ez(u) * tot`, per-segment heat from `age = t - tD[i]` (`h1=exp(-age/.05)` white-ember core, `h2=exp(-age/.32)` signal glow decaying to the kind's base colour: ash for construction, bone for primitives), dashes by arc-length phase. Group alphas (`groupAlpha`) are the "scene graph" for fading layers (construction lines fade 50% late, 'prompt' fades at B4, etc.). The pen position `penAt(t)` is a piecewise function of the stroke list (travel between strokes with `inOutCubic`, dwell on each stroke's head), so spark and drawing are the same data, never out of sync.
- **Text**: one `Layer2D`; world-space type via a transform per word/glyph (`tx()` builds a rotated, scaled matrix from the camera so roll and zoom apply to type); lyric glyph by glyph (`layout()`), karaoke via clip rects (L→R wipe; I from the baseline up), ghost outline strokes ahead of sung glyphs, Plex Mono notes with typewriter reveal and orange block cursors; fade by apparent px size (`sizeA`) so tiny notes disappear as you zoom out.
- **Spark**: `sparkHead` + `sparkParticles` drawn in a second additive `LineBatch(16000)` after the type, with a 12-sample trail of fading segments behind the pen.
- **Post**: bloom (0.72), CA tied to tremor, small shake/punch/flash on beats, `frame` (crop marks).

## 1.4 Why it's impressive
1. **One data model drives everything**: the same list of timed strokes produces the drawing, the pen/spark position, the typed TikZ listing, the hot-to-cool trail glow and the dimension labels, so there is never drift between "what is drawn" and "what the spark does". The pen *has to* travel between strokes and the code gives it plotter motion (ease + travel time), which is what sells the "plotter pen" read.
2. **Words are physics events**: "I" is drawn as a rectangle and fills from the baseline up; "sparks" throws sparks per glyph; "AGI" is three letter-slams on the three sung syllables fed by 110/45/45 horn sparks; "eyes" is a dot with `r = 0.08`; "nervous" is a tremor envelope on everything; "surprise" is `−log p` rolling to 0.00. Each lyric word is turned into a literal action on the artefact.
3. **Camera as drum fill**: ~23 keys, mostly snapped on beat-aligned times with `outExpo`: a 4x pull-back on the downbeat (2.056), a 0.29 s snap onto the horn/AGI with roll (3.647), a 0.48 s dive onto the eye, a 3.7x snap-out on "Your", a 7-unit whip on "that's". The camera zooms about the move's fixed point, in log space.
4. **Transformation chain**: pen-plot → hatching → PCB traces → ckpt morph (with the 5-legged pony) → vanishing into black → the same spark restarts as the loss curve. Visual language changes four times in 9 s without a cut.

## 1.5 Honest observations (why it may feel weaker than the code sounds)
- Bulk of the frame is graph-paper dark; the unicorn is small (about 1/3 frame height in the 2.1–3.6 s shots) and the lyric is a small block in a corner until AGI. The strongest 1.5 s (3.65–5.0) is the only time type and drawing are large together.
- Much of the detail (dimension values, LSB/RSB, protractor ticks) is 6–13 px: invisible at normal viewing and only discovered by pausing. The original pays for those details with hidden craft; they do not "read".
- The weak-point we should avoid is the *middle*: 5.9–7.7 s is a slow PCB state (a static pony with tiny table, text swapping) with only the tremor as motion. What holds it is the beat-snapped morph on 6.147 / 6.601 and the vibration.

## 1.6 Reusable techniques
- **Pen-as-data plotter**: define every mark as `{points, t0, t1, ease, kind}`, invert the ease to get `tD[i]`, derive both the drawn head and the spark position from the same list. Gives matched "being drawn" glow (`exp(-age/τ)` hot-to-cool) for free. Pen travel between strokes with `inOutCubic` and `clamp(0.08 + 0.025 d, .08, .32)`.
- **Type as the drawing target**: measure the glyph (box, bearings, advance) and draw dimension lines/labels on it; one glyph ("I") equals a primitive (rectangle). Karaoke fill direction can encode the object (I fills like a rectangle).
- **Syllable-driven hits**: use `word.syl` timing to fire sparks, letter pops, camera snaps on each sung syllable (AGI = 3 hits).
- **Log-space camera keys with fixed-point zoom** for dive/pull-back gestures; roll keys of +-0.02 to 0.07 rad on snaps; `outExpo` for snaps, `inOutCubic` for dives; punch zoom `0.02*pulse(.08)` on hits.
- **Octilinear PCB router** (resample, snap to grid, diagonal+straight, 3 offset tracks, vias, pulses on 8ths): an automatic "turn any drawing into circuit traces" transform.
- **Handoff to the next scene by matching a screen position through black** (compute the next scene's first-frame spark position in the current scene's code).

## 2.2 Rendering technique
- **Terrain**: a 44 x 42 plane mesh (640 x 720 segments) displaced in the vertex shader by an analytic height function (`terrH`: sin-sum hills, a meandering canyon `cuF(v)`, a bowl, and a sharp 1/r pit `-1.3/sqrt(r^2+eps^2)*exp(-r^2/2.6^2)`), with the **identical function in TS** (`terrH`) for camera collision, canyon centreline, spark position and labels. Fragment shader: contour lines `contour(y/interval)` with `fwidth`-scaled width and a fade to mean tone when the lines get denser than ~3 px (anti-moire), minor every `DEC/10`, index every `DEC/2`; **Tanaka-style illuminated contours** (brightness by slope aspect vs light direction, `tanaka`), hachures running down the slopes (stagger per band, gaps at each contour; radial in the pit), fog by distance, a reveal radius with a bright wavefront, edge falloff, `uPulse` (4 heights) for beat pulses travelling down the levels from the spark. The spark's trail and labels are 3D `LineBatch` and Canvas2D projected by the same `viewProjection` matrix.
- **Labels**: cartographic elevation labels placed on the contour lines by Newton projection onto the nearest level (`onContour`), rotated along the contour tangent with an ink backing plate; `local min.` markers found by gradient descent from a grid of seeds; **two elevation labels read `P(doom) 0.0x`** (signal colour) = the PDoom cameo in the plate's own idiom.
- **Type**: Canvas2D with 3D-projected glyphs on the canyon (per-glyph scale/rotate so letters stand on the floor).
- **Post**: bloom .7, shake, flash, CA, vignette rising with `tens`.

## 2.3 Why it's impressive
- A **graph becomes a world**: the chart's own log axis becomes the vertical scale of the terrain, the curve's cliff becomes the actual drop, the camera goes out through the chart floor with no cut. The metaphor (training loss → loss landscape → minimum) is literal geometry.
- The lyric is written *on* the data: on the curve (text on path), then on the canyon floor in perspective, then as typography that flips on "boss": every line has its own idiom.
- Dry jokes carried in tiny type (`grokking (?)`, `∆loss −99.9999%`, `sharp minimum (generalizes poorly)`, `P(doom) 0.03` as a contour label) while the main read is big type.
- Camera work: a dolly-in on the beat before "sudden", fall, orbit, reframe on beat, cut on the downbeat, a 180 deg roll on "boss" with a 3.5x dolly and a 30 degree FOV opening into the cut; all keyed to beat positions found by lyric/beat lookups.

## 2.4 Reusable techniques
- **Chart-to-terrain dive**: a 2D chart plane in a perspective scene at z=0; at the cliff time the spark free-falls (gravity solved from landing time), the camera lerps position/target/roll along with it, and a procedural height field (same function in JS and GLSL) is revealed beneath by a radial wave. Keep the chart's log scale = terrain scale.
- **Procedural contour shader** with `fwidth` anti-moire, index/minor contours, slope-aspect illumination (Tanaka), hachures; beat pulses as `exp(-|y - level|*2.4)` bands travelling down.
- **Text on path in 3D**: project path points, orient glyphs by projected tangent, size by perspective.
- **World roll with a 2D overlay**: rotate the 3D camera roll and the canvas layer by the same angle; draw the second half of the sentence rotated by pi so it comes right-side up with the flip.
- **Beat anchoring by lookup**: choose cut/move times as `nearestBeat(word.start +- offset)` inside a window so camera hits land on the grid.
- **SGD zig-zag**: spark path = centreline + damped sinusoids.

# 3. `prompt` x3 — the pre-chorus prompt field (`chatgpt` 16.601–22.509, `sydney` 52.508–58.871, `gato` 88.870–95.233)

One scene file, three variants. The plea is typed **as LLM tokens** on the sung word starts, each token with a mini next-token distribution above it; the line ends with a ⏎ that launches the chorus. Joke candidate tables are in `prompt-data.ts`.

## 3.1 Common machinery (all three)
- **Layers**: a Canvas2D `ui` layer (1920x1080 UI plane drawn through a camera transform `setTransform(cs, sn, -sn, cs, ...)` with zoom, rotation, centre) composited in a variant-specific fragment shader (`overUI`: `col = mix(bg, ui.rgb * (1 + hotBoost * hot), ui.a)` with `hot = smoothstep(.25, .7, r - 1.3 g)`, so only the signal-orange pixels overshoot the bloom threshold; the type glows orange, everything else stays flat). A small additive `LineBatch` draws the caret spark / smile spark on top.
- **Field**: ink slot (alpha .9), bone hairline rect (.42), four corner registration ticks, `›` prompt glyph, fine print below the field in 13 px Plex Mono: `PROMPT 01` (3 px tracking, number in signal), `T 0.7 · top-p 0.95 · seed 0x2A`, **`P(doom) 0.04 · context-dependent`** (the live `PDoom.value(t)`: the plate's cameo), `CONTEXT 08 / 8192` (counts tokens typed), `⏎ send  (irreversible)`; a keycap with return arrow at the right: outline pulses in sync with a 2 Hz cosine once armed (`t >= tLast + .1`), fills signal at ⏎ and presses to 0.9 scale (`0.5^(dt/.12)`).
- **Tokens**: Plex Mono 400 at 60 px (fixed advance), `plain()` typewriter quotes. Each word is split per `SPECS` into tokens (`ChatGPT,` = `Chat | G | P | T,`; the split uses the aligned syllable times `syl` [16.6, 17.24, 17.92, 18.36] so the tokens are typed on the *sung syllables*), every token at its own `t0`. On arrival: drop `-10 px * (1 - outExpo(age/.18))`; colour `ember` for `0.5^(age/.06)` (flash), then signal while the word is being sung, then cooling to bone over 0.35 s after `word.end`. A hairline **token bracket** under each token (with end ticks) carries a fake 4-digit token id (10 px mono, `1000 + hash*98000`). The word's karaoke is a 3 px signal bar that grows along the brackets and fades.
- **Distribution popup** ("p( next | context )"): a 290 px panel above the token (leader line to the token), appears **`POP_PRE = 0.16 s` before the token lands** in the "computing..." state (rows flicker on/off with probability `.25 + .75*built`, bar lengths jitter), flips to "sampled" at `t0`: the picked row gets a signal fill flash `0.14 + .5*0.5^((t-t0)/.08)`, orange text, a drawn triangle marker; other rows dim to ash. Rows show text, bar `80 px * p/pmax`, probability (2 decimals). Held 0.62 s (0.9 s in gato), longer for sustained words (`word.end - t0 - .45`), then collapses (`scaleY inCubic`, 0.12 s) before the next popup starts. Candidates are jokes (`Chat .61 / Claude .12 / Siri .04 / Mom .02`; `eat .44 / delete .21 / train on .18 / rate .05`; `alive .52 / first .18 / gently .11 / later .09`). For "don't" the **sampled** token is the *less* probable one (`do .46`, `don't .38`, `pick: 1`): the sample is never the argmax in the joke.
- **Caret**: 3.5 px signal bar, on while a token is fresh (< .45 s) or on beat phase < .5 (blinks at the tempo); a small spark head (`sparkHead(…, 0.5*sqrt(zoom), .35*0.5^(since/.1))`) flares at the caret for 0.3 s after every token.
- **Camera = hard cuts on sung words**, with a slow creep per shot (`zoom = shot.zoom * (1 + push*(t - shot.t))`), and a micro kick per token (`zoom *= 1 + .012*0.5^((t-t0)/.07)`) and a small beat pulse (`.004*(1-beatPhase)^5`).
- **⏎ launch time** is on the grid: `tEnter = first beat (gato: 8th) after the last word + gap`, never later than `end - 0.2`: chatgpt **22.055** (beat 48 = the downbeat where chorus 1 begins in the music), sydney **57.962** (beat 127), gato **94.778** (beat 208). So the press lands on the music's section hit, while the visual scene continues until the next downbeat.

## 3.2 `chatgpt` (16.601–22.509): "throat"
- **Background**: a fragment shader rendering an **engraved throat / tunnel** seen down its length (it reads as concentric contour rings in the renders: the continuation of `loss`'s dive into the minimum): polar `q = rot(t*.05)*(p - vp)`, radius `R = r / wob` with an organic wobble `1 + .045 snoise(dir*.9 + t*.05) + .02 snoise(...)`, depth `z = .5/R`, along-throat coord `u = z*2.3 + camZ` (one ring per unit); cartilage ridge `pow(.5 + .5 cos(TAU(fu - .5)), 4)` lit by a headlamp with fog `exp(-.2 z)`; hairlines drawn in `u*11` space with constant pixel width (fades to average tone when dense: anti-moire), plus short broken longitudinal folds (`ang = a/TAU*220 + noise`), a deep ember glow at the vanishing point `heat() * exp(-r*11)`. `camZ = lt*.42 + 2.2*(lt/dur)^2.4 + 16*rushK^2.2`: it creeps toward the pit slowly, then **rushes** at ⏎. A **swallow** wave: on "eat" (20.27) `swallow` ramps 0→1.1 over 0.9 s and a contraction wave (`exp(-(z - wz)^2/2)`, z compressed 18%, brightness +1.4*wave) travels from the far end toward the camera.

## 3.3 `sydney` (52.508–58.871): bars and a smile

## 3.4 `gato` (88.870–95.233): the quiet breakdown
- Background: near-black, a faint ash pool of light (`.035 glow`), sparse **motes drifting up slowly** (hash grid 18 cells, 14% lit, drift `.12/s`, wobble `.08`). - **Camera: no cuts**, only long eases (blend 1.2–1.6 s `inOutCubic`): zoom 1.6 → 1.45 → 1.2 → 1.0 → 0.9 (+ noise drift `22 px * noise(.23t)`, bob `14 px`, rot `.012`).
- **Letters drift apart** ("don't let me go"): after each word ends + 0.35 s, each glyph accumulates `k = age^1.3 * smoothstep(0, .8, age)`, with `dx = -D*.045*k` (D = distance from the last letter: letters slide *toward* it, spreading the line from the held letter), `dy = ((hash - .5)*34 - 10*D/900)*k + sin(.8t + 1.7i)*2`, rotation `(hash - .5)*.16*k*min(1, D/200)`: the sentence slowly disintegrates into loose letters. ## 3.5 Why it's impressive (prompt)
- One tiny idiom (a text field with token boundaries and a probability panel) carries three distinct moods through background + camera only: **throat** (swallowing, rushing, energy up), **cage** (bars closing per beat, smile), **dark and alone** (no cuts, drifting letters).
- It stages LLM internals as UI: token boundaries with ids, sampling, **the sampled token is not always the most likely**, `CONTEXT n / 8192`, `send (irreversible)`, and P(doom) in the fine print with a different qualifier each time (`context-dependent`, `mood-dependent`, `cat-dependent`).
- The ⏎ press is *on the section's downbeat* so the visual launch and the chorus drop are the same event, hidden in a 0.45 s rush + white-out for the hook.

## 3.6 Reusable techniques
- **Next-token panel**: "computing..." flicker for 0.16 s before the sample lands, then a flash on the chosen row; candidates are the joke carriers; bars are `p/pmax`.
- **Typing synced to syllables**: split a word into tokens, time each to its sung syllable (`word.syl`), fall back to `min(.09, dur/n)`.
- **Camera cut list keyed to word indices** (`ws(i)`), with a small push per shot and token-arrival kick.
- **Per-beat discrete notch animation** `steps = floor(b) + outExpo(frac*5)` (bars, counters).
- **Throat/tunnel shader**: `z = .5/R`, `u = z*k + camZ`, hairlines in `u`, anti-moire fade, ember vanishing point.
- **Letter dispersal**: per-glyph age-power drift away from an anchor with the caret following the anchor.

# 4. `hook` x4 — "I'M / UPPING / MY / P(DOOM)" (22.509, 58.871, 95.233, 124.322; each ~1.4–1.8 s)

Per hook there are 4 word slams (each exactly on the sung word start) then the **P(doom) instrument takes the frame** (rolling digits from the previous value to the new one) and leaves on the cut in a way that hands geometry to the next plate. ## 4.1 Shared motion rules
- **Slam**: `scale = hold*(1 + amt*(1 - outExpo((t - t0)/.16)))`, `hold = 1 + .035*(t - t0)` (a held word keeps creeping toward the camera), `amt` .16 for I'M, .12 for MY and P(DOOM) (.3/.3/.25 in hook 4). - **UPPING rises**: each letter starts below the frame (`H + 1.3 capH`) and goes to the baseline with `outExpo` over `dur = min(.3, hold*.8)`, staggered `min(.035, hold*.08)` per letter; vertical stretch `1 + clamp(vel/5000, 0, 1.3)` from the actual velocity (finite difference at t-1/60), and 4 ghost outline strokes trail behind (`vel*.012 px` spacing). Held words keep drifting up at 70 px/s (900 px/s in hook 4).

## 4.6 Why it's impressive
- A **single typographic slam recurs four times and escalates by changing the rules, not the content**: clean → colour-inverted → whisper (hairline/tiny/slow) → strobing/echoing: the third is the loudest by being quiet.
- **The number is the climax of the hook, not a corner HUD**: digit drums, motion-blurred, bar and footnote jokes, and each hook's exit transforms the number into the next plate's first image (spark → outline, eyelid seam, burn-out, weft thread).
- Everything lands on word starts with 2-frame inversion punches and punchy shake/zoom; fits to full width (`fitSize`) regardless of word.

## 4.7 Reusable techniques
- **Word-start slam**: `s = 1 + amt*(1 - outExpo((t - t0)/.16))`, 2/60 s colour inversion, shake/zoom pulses, held words creeping in scale.
- **Rising stretch type from velocity**: take the finite difference of the letter position, stretch Y by velocity, draw ghost strokes behind.
- **Odometer digits**: continuous value → drum positions with carry; blur copies when the drum is fast; detent on the last digit.
- **Math-set label** (italic P, light stretched parens, heavy word) as a visual idiom for "P(doom)".
- **Hand-off geometry**: finish each exit in the exact position/shape/scale of the next plate's first frame (spark point, eye seam, thread line) and keep the camera still for the last frames.
- **Escalation by rule changes**: palette strobe on 8ths, per-beat re-slams, outline echoes, digits that multiply.
