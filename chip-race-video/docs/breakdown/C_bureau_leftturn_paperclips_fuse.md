# Group C breakdown: `bureau`, `leftturn`, `paperclips`, `fuse`

Source: mexicat/pdoom-video `app/src/scenes/{bureau,leftturn,leftturn-gantt,leftturn-map,paperclips,paperclips-geo,paperclips-glsl,fuse,fuse-pitch}.ts`. Windows from `timeline.ts` + `data/*.json` (song seconds, 132 bpm, beat = 0.4545 s, bar = 1.818 s): bureau 69.78-81.14, leftturn 81.14-88.87, paperclips 96.60-102.05, fuse 102.05-109.78. Contact sheets and dense stills I rendered live in `pdoom-video/out/wip/C/`. Eases are the names in `engine/util.ts`; `pulse(t,t0,hl)` = instant hit decaying with half-life `hl` (used for every shake/punch), `prog(t,a,b,ease)` = eased 0..1 ramp.

Common to all four: every move is quoted from lyric word starts (`word.start`, `syl[]` syllable splits) or the nearest beat/downbeat, never hard-coded seconds. Camera = a pure function `camAt(t)`; motion blur is real (multi-tap in the shader or sub-frames), never a post blur.

### Rendering technique
- **Channel-coded ink -> paper shader.** All ink is drawn on one Canvas2D layer with `globalCompositeOperation='lighter'` on black: **R = typewriter ink, G = printed (press) ink, B = orange ink**. `PAPER_FRAG` samples it and composites on procedural paper: low-freq fbm cloud (0.0021), mid snoise, **random-oriented fibre segments** (3x3 hashed cells of 22 page-px, two octaves, +-5 % luminance), rare specks, raking-light gradient, **binder punch holes** (r 17 at x -770, y +-250: see-through to the dark desk with a lit lower rim), sheet vignette. Ink is **Beer-Lambert overprint** (`col *= pow(transmission, density)` per ink), so orange over black print multiplies like real overprinting. Typewriter ink has per-char density 0.8-1.0, noise modulation and fibre drop-out; each char gets hashed jitter (+-0.8 px x, +-1.2 px y, +-0.01 rad) and a 4 px strike bounce (`pulse hl .04`).
- **Stamps = shader texture on a stamp mask.** `stampMask` (rotated rect) switches the orange/black channel to `stampInk`: three octaves of snoise (0.07/0.23/0.9) thresholded into **voids**, a very-low-freq "press pressure" gradient (0.0045), mottling 0.72-1.0, and **heavier edges** (coverage minus a 4-tap blur x2). `strength` starts at 1.5 and decays over 0.05 s so the first frame is overinked and fills in. SAFE ENOUGH (Archivo 75w/900 fitted to the double border, 12 px + 3.5 px rules, mono microtype "EVALUATED - WE RECKON") is orange; FILED is black. Both are drawn as normal vector art; the texture is applied in the shader.
- **Motion blur on the paper**: 32-tap directional blur along `blurV`, = camera centre displacement over 1/60 s, only applied if |d|>40 px (length `min(170, 0.5*len)`), never across cuts. Whips and the carriage return smear; everything else is crisp.
- **Burn pass** (`BURN_FRAG`): the page is two planes, each hinged on a vertical at `lineX +- 1250` px, seen with a pinhole (CAMD 2200) and swung by `open + wake*smoothstep(0,700,y-front)`; a 5-iteration fixed-point inverse finds the page pixel under each screen pixel. Burnt gap = pointed tip at the spark (half width 5 px) widening behind (5+11(1-e^{-back/180}) + two snoise octaves of raggedness); scorch (browned paper 0.58,0.40,0.27, 5-23 px band), black **char band** 1.6-5 px, glowing rim with `heat` (white-hot tip -> signal -> blood -> out, flicker via noise(time*6)). The spark's light is added **through the paper where there is no ink** (`clear = luma(pg)/luma(bone)`) so the diagram's black lines silhouette against the glow before the break-through. Page slides down at `slideK 1.25x` the roadmap's slide (it is nearer the camera: parallax).
- Post: `paper:1` (HUD switches to ink), bloom 0.2 at threshold 1.8 (so only the burn/spark blooms), grain 0.045, ca 0.5.

### Why it's impressive
- Bureaucracy is built as *physics of ink and paper* (overprint, fibre, stamp voids), not as a flat "paper texture": the stamp looks pressed, not placed. The 30 px per-frame shake with a 70 ms half-life gives a real *thump* on one frame.
- The typewriter is the camera: it steps with every keystroke, then the carriage return is a blurred whip. The cut to the stamp lands on the first frame of the word.
- Mirrored "backward," and a *time-remapped* "repeat" are the lyric made literal; the epoch counter jumping to 1,000,000 is the payoff gag.
- The hand-off: the next scene's spark is already lighting the page from behind a full beat early, then burns the page in two on a downbeat, with the halves in perspective. Zero dissolve.

### Reusable techniques
- **Channel-coded ink layer + shader paper**: draw all marks into R/G/B as semantic ink types, composite once with per-ink Beer-Lambert transmission and per-ink grain. One canvas, believable print.
- **Stamp slam recipe**: scale 1.07->1 in 50 ms `outQuad`, rotation ~-7 deg, initial overinking that decays, per-frame hashed shake with half-life 70 ms, 5 % zoom pulse, hard camera cut on the same frame.
- **Remap-time stutter**: build the whole shot as `draw(tRemapped)`; for N repeats `tr = lerp(src0, src1, u)` per segment, camera derived from `tr`, but pops/ants driven by real `t`. Increment a counter per loop; hold at `src1`.
- **Mirrored typeset**: `layout()` the word, draw glyph i at `x0 - g.x` with `scale(-1,1)`; progress the reveal in reverse reading order.
- **Burn-in-two transition**: render the next plate under the page, light it through the paper's un-inked areas, cut along the spark's path with a pointed-tip SDF gap + char band, swing halves on vertical hinges with a tiny perspective solve.

### Rendering technique
- **One shader, whole world** (`MAP_FRAG`): `toWorld(FRAG_PX, cam)` inverts the camera+keystone; per tap it shades: drafting grid at 40/200 units (`gridCov` with min 0.8 px lines), **terra incognita contours** as iso-lines of a height field `h = terrain(w) + faceH(p) + wave + beat ripples` using `fwidth(h)`-based AA (`pxLine`), minor + index (every 5th) + fine quarter sub-contours on the face, density fade where lines get closer than ~2 px, hillshade from NW; mask relief from `_motifs.MASK` (dome, 2 pits, arc groove). Static map (route, milestones, title block "ROADMAP - AGI, v1.0 / REV C SUPERSEDES REV B (ALSO FINE)", +-3 sigma schedule envelope) is a 4096^2 Canvas2D texture; road paint is a separate RGBA marks texture; the spark's burnt **trail** is computed analytically from the route SDF (`route(p)` returns distance and arc length; `uTrail` = spark arc length; heat fades by `exp(-age/420)`).
- **World overlay canvas** (`WorldLayer`): per frame, labels/Gantt are drawn on a 2880x1620 canvas with the camera's affine transform (world units, so hairlines are `px = 1/zoom`), then *sampled by the map shader through the same camera + keystone + blur*. Result: text and vector UI share the perspective and motion blur with the map for free.
- Spark: additive `LineBatch` `sparkHead` + `sparkParticles` (rate 120, speed 280 along the route; on the Gantt rate 110/50 when stalled).
- "The face breathes on the beat": per beat two ripple rings (radius 1500 u/s, amp 0.9, e^{-t/0.45}) from the face centre light the contours with bone-ember; eyes flare on kicks (`hit('kick',t,0.1)`).

### Why it's impressive
- The turn is a 90-degree camera roll with a 16-tap shutter *and* road paint that reads in perspective, so the whip feels like a car, not a pan.
- The reveal re-reads the same pixels: the crater you braked into is an eye; the contours you were driving over are a smiley. Zero new assets, just a camera crane + height-field iso-lines.
- The Gantt makes time literal: bars are exactly as long as each word is sung; the playhead has to wait at an empty slot; the slot becomes the next scene's caret.

### Reusable techniques
- **Map as one shader + camera model with keystone and N-tap shutter**: interpolate camera pose between t and t-shutter and loop the shade function 1..16 times by `motion/3`; deterministic and sub-frame correct.
- **World-anchored overlay canvas sampled by a shader**: put UI/labels through the same projection as the world.
- **Iso-line topography from any scalar field** with `fwidth` AA and density fade; add `wave` terms for shockwaves and beat ripples.
- **Gantt-as-lyric**: x = `(t - t0) * V`; bar = [word.start, word.end]; fill with `wordProgress`; milestone stamps on beats; playhead clamps to `min(X(t), slotX)`.
- **Slot-to-caret morph**: interpolate half-widths of a diamond polygon to a thin rectangle; align to the next scene's first element in screen space.

### Rendering technique
- **Engraved wire shading** (`shadeWireL`): the clip is a tube defined by an analytic 2D distance to its centreline (4 straight legs + 3 half-circle bends, `clipDL` without trig). Height `z = sqrt(r^2 - d^2)`, normal = `(offset, z)`; **bone hairlines run along the wire** (`u = theta/pi*7+0.5` around the tube), coverage by `hatchW(u, tone^1.5*1.15, fw)` where line width swells with light, plus a crisp spec line `smoothstep(.3,.6,pow(N.H,60))` and an **orange rim-light** from the back (only when the wire is >3-12 px wide). When the hatch is sub-pixel the coverage blends to flat grey (`mix(l, darkness, smoothstep(0.3,0.75,fw))`), which kills moire.
- **Infinite lattice via layer-stacked SDF**: per ray step only 2 layers are evaluated (`k0`, `k0+1`) from an infinite stack at `z = -k*pz`; each layer is basket-woven 40-unit cells (alternating 0/90 deg, 4 clips per cell), per-layer random offset, per-clip jitter (+-1.5 u, +-0.15 z, +-0.04 rad, 10 % missing), brightness `0.5^k` so deeper layers fall into black. Bounding-box test before `clipDL`; 128 steps, step = `max(0.9d, pixelFootprint*(0.2+0.003t))`, hit at 0.1 px; soft shadow (28 steps) + AO (5 taps) only where the wire is > 6-16 px wide (`nearK`); 4-tap RGSS supersampling (one tap/sub-frame in export via `SS_TAP`). A second stack from above (`ceilZ`) gives the ceiling; the empty slab between is skipped analytically.
- Fog: exponential + a horizon haze `INK2*exp(-|rd.z|*18)`. Post: bloom 0.65, vignette 0.45, grain 0.06 and CA at 100.05-101.5 visible as RGB fringing on wire edges.
- Phase A and B-D are two different shaders cross-fed by the same camera struct; the top-down view is exact orthographic-like (camera over plane), so the CPU `items(t)` splitting and the GPU lattice flood share coordinates and the join is invisible.

### Why it's impressive
- The metaphor is *built* rather than shown: the one line becomes one clip, it doubles on 8th notes, and the doubling becomes the floor you are then flying over. Quiet, hypnotic, then claustrophobic on the beat.
- The lyric is the geometry: "nowhere left to go" is squashed to the actual height of the slot between ceiling and floor; the last frame is that slot reduced to a single glowing line.
- Engraving-style shading on a raymarched scene gives a look no PBR shader has; moire is solved with footprint-aware hatch.

### Reusable techniques
- **Doubling on subdivisions**: split k at `timeOfBeat(b+1+0.5k)` with `outExpo` slides along alternating axes; rotate copies a quarter-turn so the pattern becomes a basket weave rather than a tile.
- **Analytic-centreline wire SDF + fake tube normal** for top-down hero shots, same function raymarched in 3D (`sqrt(d2^2+z^2)-r`).
- **Infinite 2-layer stack march** (evaluate only the two nearest layers) for endless fields with depth fade `0.5^k`.
- **Squeezable lyric**: Archivo width 62 + non-uniform canvas scale where `sy` follows the live gap geometry.
- **Slit-close masking** (`smoothstep(slitH+30, slitH, |y-horizon|)`) as a match-cut handle into a horizontal line.

### Rendering technique
- **Fuse cord shader** (`FUSE_FRAG`): a ribbon mesh with attributes (arc length s, across v in [-1.45,1.45]); braid = two helix families `a1 = s/pitch + asin(v)/pi*3`, `a2 = s/pitch - asin(v)/pi*3` (pitch `1.55*rad`) alternating over/under by `mod(floor(a1)+floor(a2),2)`; `bulge = sin(pi*across)`, `dive` smoothsteps; engraved white-line hatch on top (`hatch(across*4+.5+noise, light*.95)`), key from above (dark underside), **ember light ahead of the front** `warmL = exp(-ds/(7 rad))` tinting the cord. Behind the front (`burnt = smoothstep(3,-26,ds)`) the cord shrinks to 0.58+-0.2 noise, breaks into gaps far behind, becomes graphite ash with crack hatch and **dying embers** `heat(.35+.55 e^{ds/70})*flicker`; at the front a white-hot band `exp(-|ds+4|/9)*(3.2,1.5,0.55)` and a halo.
- Ground: ink with 7 px engraved hatch only revealed in the spark's pool of light (`exp(-d^2/(430^2 zoom))`) + fbm smoke rising from the burnt trail. Chart ground: graph paper (24/120 px rulings) only lit near the spark: *the spark is the only light source of the whole plate*.
- **String simulation** (`initString`, run once at init at 1 kHz, sampled with lerp so frames stay pure functions of t): pitch from `fuse-pitch.ts`: pYIN of the lead vocal stem, 100 Hz, MIDI numbers anchored to `blues.start`: held ~C4 (60.2) with ~7 Hz vibrato +-0.4 semitone, then a glissando 62 -> 72 in ~0.2 s (the octave flip, = 276 px), B-flat 70, then G-F (67-65). Short unvoiced gaps (<70 ms) are interpolated. While voiced a stiff "finger" drives displacement D to `max(0, pitch - 59.1) * 23 px` (x0.55 after the word ends) with a 2nd-order spring (**26 Hz natural, zeta 0.95**); when unvoiced the string rings freely (**5.2 Hz, zeta 0.09**). Shape of a string pushed at x=1060 between anchors (470, 1650): soft-min of two ramps, `-k ln(e^{-l/k}+e^{-r/k})`, k=0.17 (rounded apex). `stringY(x) = 540 - D*shape(x)`.
- Post: bloom 0.8 (threshold 1.05, knee 0.35), halation 0.3, vignette 0.4 (0.25 on the chart), grain 0.05. Plate is monochrome bone + signal; chart plate lit only by the spark (revision 2 removed the ultramarine).

### Why it's impressive
- The burn is **written to the voice**: the front reaches each word on its start, the held "fuse" burns letter by letter for 2 s. The char-to-ash and crumble give a full life cycle in one word.
- The orthogonality chart is a joke made by the graph itself: the flat r=0.00 regression line becomes a guitar string, "blues" literally stands on it, and the bend you see is the singer's measured pitch. Vibrato wobbles it; the octave leap lifts it by 276 px; the blue note gets a lone orange flat.
- The spark never leaves: fuse -> axis -> pitch carrier -> lands on the next plate's axis.

### Reusable techniques
- **Burn schedule from lyric knots**: front arc length = piecewise linear through `(word.start, firstGlyph.s)`; invert it to get each glyph's ignition time (`timeAtBurn`); lerp hot->ember->signal->blood->ash and crumble with destination-out polygon bites.
- **Text on a path with arc-length lookup** (`pointAtLength` + tangent) with per-glyph state.
- **Audio pitch -> physical string**: pre-extract pitch (pYIN) to a 100 Hz array, anchor to a lyric word, drive a spring-damper offline into a table, sample in render; ghost afterimages at -12/-26 ms fake vibration.
- **Vertical world-to-world slide** with a feathered edge and spark lerp = cheap, readable "camera tilts down" between two different render modes.
- **Beat-synchronised sputter**: second particle emitter active only while `beatPhase<0.1`.
