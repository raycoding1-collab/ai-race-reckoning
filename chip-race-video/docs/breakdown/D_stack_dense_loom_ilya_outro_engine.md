## PLATE `stack`: "Architecture (recursive)" (109.778 to 115.232)

### 1. Shot list and motion spec
Whole plate = one continuous 3D fall through an infinite stack of transformer blocks, camera falls one block per beat, 4 camera "setups" that change on step times, then a dead stop.

Fog scale 0.75 to 1 over the same crane. The fall begins with `kind:'drop'`: 4 blocks in one move, `amt*ease.outExpo(clamp(dt/0.42))` (fast then slow in 0.42 s). First frame (sheet 109.80) is a vertiginous receding tunnel of block tops.
- **Beat steps, 110.687, 111.141, 111.596, 112.05, 112.505, 112.959, 113.414, 113.868 (8 beats).** One block per beat, `P = 7.6` world units per block pitch. ### 2. Rendering technique
- 3D hairline CAD: ~1000 3D segments per block (`buildTemplate`: chamfered front face, back face, depth edges, lamination lines, inner frame, MULTI-HEAD ATTENTION box with 3 head-offset ghost edges, ADD & NORM, FEED FORWARD, residual bypasses with (+) nodes, Q K V arrows, labels in the single-stroke `tech` font, registration crosses, dimension bracket, "L.nnn" layer labels) rendered through **`LineBatch(90000, {screen2D:false, depthTest:true})`** with a PerspectiveCamera. ~18 blocks are live (`k0 = focus-3 .. focus+14`), alpha-faded by a fog function `exp(-(dz - dist*0.85)/(dist*1.1*fogScale))`.
- **Hidden-line removal trick:** each block also draws a solid dark slab (`InstancedMesh` of `BoxGeometry` with `MeshBasicMaterial` at ink/ink2, scene fog) *into the same depth buffer* before the lines, so lines behind a block are hidden and the diagram reads as solid objects.
- **Custom motion blur on top of the engine's:** during moves, geometry is drawn `NS = ceil(|dy|/0.1)` (up to 12) times smeared along the fall by `dy = (pos(t) - pos(t - 0.9/60)) * P` (clamped +-1.8 world), each at `alpha/NS*1.6`, brightness `speedDim = 1/(1+|dyRaw|*0.25)`. Words are smeared with up to 9 copies. This is a stylised "speed" streak independent of the export's sub-frame blur.
- Words: `TextPlane` (stack-kit.ts) = Canvas2D text texture on a 3D plane, red channel = fill, green channel = stroke outline; fragment shader does the karaoke wipe in `uv.x` with `prog`, `dir`, `feather 0.08`, unsung = bone outline (aDim 0.45) and ghost fill (0.04), sung = signal (`cSung`) cooling instantly to bone at `w.end+0.03` ("a linear blend reads salmon"), additive `flash` uniform = `pulse(t,w.start,0.07)*1.4` white-hot. Archivo width 75, weight 900 for every word, one word-group per block, rows greedily wrapped (max width 12.6 world).
- Background `FSPass`: ink + haze lighter at the bottom ("the stack never ends") + a faint drafting grid that parallaxes with the fall + hashed falling streaks.
- Post: `bloom 0.65, ca 1.6, vignette 0.42` + the exposure pulse. Everything else default.
- HUD canvas 2D: DEPTH gauge (mono 15/30 px), "N × transformer block, N → ∞", P(DOOM) cameo in orange mono under the gauge (0.81 value at this point).

### 3. Why it's impressive
- A fall *that is the lyric's own structure*: "turtles all the way down" is literally an endless stack, the word per block is the quote, and the held note becomes echo-copies across the blocks it covers.
- Beat-locked physics, not keyframes: anticipation to 22%, spring to 100%, land pulse + spark pass per beat; then the one place the metre is *broken* (dead stop, telephoto, silence of the grid) is exactly "Till you learned to disobey".
- The disobey gag is three simultaneous wrongs: one block rotated 28.6° with a measured Δθ callout, the word's highlight running backwards, letters dropping out of line.

### 4. Reusable techniques
- **Solid-slab hidden-line wireframe**: draw dark instanced boxes into the depth buffer, then depth-tested `LineBatch` lines; gives engraving-look solids with no shading.
- **Anticipate + spring + beat-grid steps**: `pos(t)=Σ amt*(LEAD + (1-LEAD)*springStep(dt,3.6,0.74))` plus an `inQuad` pre-move; steps come from `beatsIn(audio, t0, t1)`, never hard-coded.
- **Per-word 3D text plane with shader wipe** (direction uniform, flash uniform, instant cool-down); wipe direction reversal is a one-uniform gag.
- **Camera setups as a phase table** (`phaseCuts`: id + time); each setup is a closed-form function of local time; changing setups on a beat reads as a cut.
- **Geometry smear blur** by redrawing the line set N times with a y-offset and alpha/N, so fast moves stay stylised even in the preview.

### 2. Rendering technique (summary)
Canvas2D for all type and instruments (ONE `Layer2D`), a single fullscreen `FSPass` for the GPU floor and another for the "thing" + grid, a 256-segment `LineBatch` only for additive glow on the fences. Everything is analytic in `t`; the only per-frame random is `hash(frameIdx(t), n)` for shake.

### 3. Why it's impressive
- The lyric is *performed as physical pressure*: each kick squeezes width, weight, leading and tracking and the screen refills; then "safety fence" is acted out on the video's own broadcast safe-area guides, with a QC log naming each violation. The metaphor is literal and the data (tokens/param 20 to 20,000) is accurate Chinchilla.
- Exponential zoom from one chip to 100,000 with the lyric spelled *by the cells*, wiped by sung progress; the count is exactly 100,000 and the counter hits 100,000 on "thousand".
- RLHF is a physics system with a *correction that fails*: the roll snaps back 55%, the reward recovers to 0.93, then it overshoots to 0.30 rad and whips to -0.12; the smiley is upside down at the end. Same stepped-spring primitive drives roll, mask offset, mask spin, type lean, reward and word slide, so everything is coupled and lags believably.
- Transitions are choreographed: stack's dead stop to flat type; the F-dive to the GPU macro; the GPU's dutch-roll start becomes askew's opening roll.

### 4. Reusable techniques
- **Width-axis squeeze with a spring masking the font-instance step**: Archivo static instances (62–125 width x 4 weights); animate by swapping instance on the beat while a horizontal `scale(sx,1)` spring (`ratio` to 1) hides the discontinuity.
- **Stepped springs**: `value = v0 + Σ (v_i - v_{i-1})*springStep(t - t_i, f, ζ)` for a value that holds then steps on beats (roll, offsets, bars). Overshoot = momentum for free.
- **Safe-area fences as characters** (title 90% / action 93% / frame): bow path with `quadraticCurveTo`, `trip()` flash, loose fragments, recoil whips.
- **Cell-mask text**: rasterise words into a small data texture, light cells by sung progress; grid pods/aisles/rows give datacentre texture; beat rings from the last 4 beats as shader uniform.
- **Dutch-angle handoff**: end one shot already rotating by `prog(t, lastBeat, cut, inCubic)*angle`, and start the next at that angle.

# ENGINE

All paths under `$REF/app/src/engine/` unless noted. Stack: TypeScript, three.js, bun + Vite; `bun scripts/render.ts` drives headless Chrome through playwright and pipes frames to ffmpeg libx264.

## E1. Scene and frame contract (`scene.ts`)
```ts
abstract class Scene {
  stateful = false; prerollMax = 6; handlesTransition = false;
  tileMasked: {rt, margin:0|1, moving?:boolean}[] = [];
  constructor(protected ctx: SceneCtx)
  init(): Promise<void>|void;  reset(); dispose();
  abstract render(f: Frame, out: THREE.WebGLRenderTarget): PostOverrides | void;
}
SceneCtx { renderer, audio: AudioData, lyrics: Lyrics, comp: Compositor, W:1920, H:1080, id, params, start, end, mapTileMask(rt, map|null) }
Frame { t, dt, lt (t-start), p (0..1), start, end, seeked, preroll,
        beat, bar (continuous indices), beatPhase, barPhase,
        a: AudioSample, under: Texture|null, tin (0..1 overlap in), tout }
```
- `render()` must fully overwrite `out` (HalfFloat linear HDR RT) and be a pure function of `f.t` (+ seeded RNG). Return `PostOverrides` = `Partial<PostParams>` (below).
- Default transition = crossfade of overlapping entries (`xfade` shader `mix(a,b,tin)`); a scene sets `handlesTransition=true` to composite `f.under` itself. The edit uses *touching windows*, i.e. hard cuts.
- Post overrides are merged **per active entry in start order**: `post = {...DEFAULT_POST, ...entry.post, ...sceneOverrides}`.

## E2. Timeline (`engine.ts`, `timeline.ts`)
`TimelineEntry { id, load: ()=>Promise<{default: SceneClass}>, start, end, caption?, post?, params?, maxSamples? }`. `Engine(canvas, makeTimeline)` calls `makeTimeline(lyrics, audio)`; `init(only?)` loads audio, lyrics, fonts, stroke fonts, instantiates `Post`, `Hud(PDoom, captions)`, then all scenes (a scene that throws in `init` renders solid red `(0.25,0,0)` and is listed in `engine.errors`). `engine.render(t, dt=1/60, toScreen, samples, shutter)` returns the sub-frame count used. `engine.reload(id)` hot-swaps (Vite HMR). Scene modules are discovered with `import.meta.glob('./scenes/*.ts')`, so an entry's file name must exist in `src/scenes/`.
`maxSamples` caps adaptive sampling while the entry is on screen (used for `shoggoth: 108`, whose half-res G-buffer sparkles).

## E3. Post chain and defaults (`post.ts`)
`DEFAULT_POST`: `exposure 1, bloom 0.55, bloomThreshold 0.85, bloomKnee 0.5, bloomRadius 0.75, halation 0.25, ca 1.2, grain 0.055, vignette 0.35, hud 1, frame 0, pdoom 0, paper 0, fade 0, flash 0, shake [0,0], zoom 1, invert 0` (+ optional `pdoomText`, `hudCorruption`).
Order of operations in the final pass (linear HDR in, 8-bit sRGB out):
1. **Zoom/shake:** `uv = (vUv - 0.5)/zoom + 0.5 - shake/res` (shake in px).
2. **Chromatic aberration** (radial, aspect-corrected): `off = dc * r2 * ca / res.x * 4` with `r2 = |dc*(aspect,1)|²`; R sampled at `uv+off`, B at `uv-off`, G centre. Magnitude ≈ 1.6·ca px at the left/right edge midpoints, ≈ 2·ca px (x) at a corner, zero at centre (cubic in radius).
3. **Bloom**: prefilter = 4-tap box downsample of the source to half-res (W>>1) + soft threshold (`rq = clamp(l - thr + knee, 0, 2knee)²/(4knee)`, `w = max(rq, l-thr)/l`, sample clamped to 40); 7-mip pyramid, 13-tap Jimenez downsample, 9-tap tent upsample accumulating `up(prev) + mip` with `radius = 0.5 + bloomRadius`; added as `col += bloom * (level0 sum) * (bloom/3)`. The pyramid stays at logical resolution at any `?scale=`.
4. **Halation**: level 3 of the up-chain (1/16 res), added as `vec3(1.0, 0.18, 0.04) * luma(halo) * halation` (red-orange film halation around highlights).
5. `col *= exposure`; HUD layer composited in linear (`mix(col, hud.rgb/a, a*hud)`) so it gets grain/vignette.
6. **Tone shoulder**: identity below k = 0.72, `k + (1-k)(1 - exp(-(x-k)/(1-k)))` above, desaturating to white `smoothstep(2,12,max)*0.85` for very bright values.
7. `invert`: `mix(col, 0.8515 - col*0.84, invert)`; `flash`: `col += C_BONE * flash` (linear, additive, so "0.02–0.04" is plenty on the hook/overflow, "1.2" is a white-out); vignette `smoothstep(0.95, 0.25, |dc*(1,0.8)|)` mixed by `vignette`; `fade` multiplies to black.
8. **Grain** (sRGB space, after tone map): `g1` per-physical-pixel hash (`hash12(gl_FragCoord.xy + fract(time*13.37)*1000)`) weight 0.6 + `g2` 2x2-logical-px cells (`fract(time*7.13)`) weight 0.4, amplitude `grain * (0.55 + 1.2*lm*(1-lm))` (strongest in mid-tones); at 4K the fine grain is multiplied by `PX_SCALE` to keep per-logical-pixel power. Then 1/255 triangular-ish dither. Grain/dither is drawn once per frame (time = song `t`), not per sub-frame.
- Per-plate values in my group: stack `bloom .65, ca 1.6, vig .42`; dense press `bloom .6, thr .95, knee .15, halation .06, vig .3, ca .7–2.3, grain .05, hud 0`; dense askew `bloom .6, thr 1.2, halation .08, vig .45, ca 1.2–3.4`; loom `bloom .8–1.4`; ilya `bloom .85–1.05, thr .9, knee .25, ca .5, vig .45, grain .06`; outro card `bloom .35, halation 0, ca 1.2–8.2`. "Only signal/ember exceed 0.85 so only they bloom; bone type stays crisp" is the key rule.
- `frame` (0..1) draws/flies out the crop marks (HUD); only bookends use it. `pdoom` > 0 shows the corner readout (default off); `paper` flips HUD ink for bone-paper plates.

## E4. Motion blur and sub-frame sampling (`engine.ts`, `render.ts`)
- **Model**: a frame at time `t` is the average of N sub-frames over a *shutter* of `shutter * dt` seconds centred on `t` (render.ts default `--shutter 0.5`; the real render recipe is `bun scripts/render.ts video --samples auto --shutter 0.2`, i.e. a 72° shutter at 60 fps = 3.33 ms window). Sub-frame k renders at `ts = max(0, t + dt*shutter*u_k)` (`u` in -0.5..0.5) through the whole `composite()` (all scenes active at `ts`, with `dt/n` as their step), is accumulated into a `FloatType` sum RT by an additive pass that **drops non-finite pixels** (`abs(c) <= 6e4` else 0, so one NaN sub-frame doesn't poison bloom), and the average is taken **before** post-processing (bloom, CA, grain see the blurred image).
- **Fixed N**: `u_k = (k+0.5)/N - 0.5`.
- **Adaptive (`--samples auto`)**: counts step 4, 12, 36, 108, 324 (`4*3^l`). `ternaryOffsets` generates the order: first 4 evenly spaced (`(i+0.5)/4 - 0.5`), each later step splits every interval in three, adding two sub-frames `(3m+0.5)/(3n)-0.5` and `(3m+2.5)/(3n)-0.5` either side of each old one, so *every prefix is evenly spread and centred*. The error after a step = how far the *displayed* (shouldered, sRGB) average moves between the old set (`sumRT*1/n`) and the new set (`newRT*1/2n`), worst 2x2 logical-px block, in 8-bit levels x170 scaling `/2` (the remaining error ≈ half the last change since stepping shrinks as 1/count). A tile stops when its and its 8 neighbours' error is below `--tol` (default 3 levels).
- **Per-tile refinement** (`refine: 'tiles'` default, tile = 32x32 logical px = 16 error blocks): converged tiles are masked into the *depth buffer* of the engine's targets (`maskPass` writes depth 1 where still refining, clears 0), and while sub-frames render, every draw into masked targets is forced to depth-test LEQUAL with no depth write (hook on three's depth state), so the GPU early-Z rejects stopped tiles. The final image is `sum * gain` with a per-tile gain texture `1/count`. `--refine frame` decides for the whole frame (all tiles at the worst count).
- In practice (docs): still frame stops at 12, normal motion 36, whips and fast zooms 108–324. Cost: scenes that draw 3D with their own depth test make that sub-frame run unmasked (the engine detects via `depthSeen`).
- **Post parameters are read at one instant**: `POST_U = 0.125`, i.e. 1/8 of the shutter after `t` (the 3rd of 4 sub-frames, a point every adaptive set includes); the sub-frame whose `u` is nearest to it donates `post` (shake, flash, zoom, fade, hud, ca...). So `post.shake` doesn't blur; shake motion blur must come from scene geometry.
- **Rules scenes must obey**: output depends only on `f.t`; stateful scenes can't use adaptive sampling (throws) and need fixed `--samples`; use `frameIdx(t) = Math.round(t*60)` (constant across the shutter) for per-frame jitter, never `Math.floor(t*60)`; a noise seeded by continuous `t` is resampled each sub-frame (averages out, slowly; costs samples); particle emitters with time-varying rate take `rate: (tb)=>..., rateMax` so identity is stable; supersampling shaders use `SS_TAP`: the engine hands each sub-frame one of 4 rotated-grid taps (`SS_TAP.value = (k + (k>>2)) % 4`) which sums to the 4-tap image at 1/4 cost; preview and single-sample stills take all 4 (`-1`).
- Stills: `render.ts stills/sheet` use `samples` too (default 1; pass `--samples auto` to see blur); the preview uses 1 sample.

## E5. Time helpers (`util.ts`)
`clamp, lerp, invLerp, remap(x,a,b,c,d,clamp=true), smoothstep(a,b,x), smootherstep, fract, mod, TAU, window01(x,a,b,fadeIn,fadeOut)`.
- `ease` (t in 0..1): `linear, inQuad, outQuad, inOutQuad, inCubic, outCubic, inOutCubic, inQuart, outQuart, inOutQuart, outQuint, inExpo, outExpo (1 - 2^(-10t), exactly 1 at t>=1), inOutExpo, outBack(t, s=1.70158), inBack, outElastic`. `outBack(t, 2.2)` is a strong overshoot (used on "disobey" letters).
- `prog(x, a, b, fn = linear) = fn(clamp((x-a)/(b-a)))` — *the* workhorse: eased, clamped progress of x through [a,b]; compose everything as `prog(t, t0, t0+dur, ease.outExpo)`.
- `keys(t, [[time, value, easeFn?], ...])`: piecewise interpolation; **the ease on key i shapes the segment ENDING at key i** (default `ease.inOutCubic`); t before the first key returns the first value, after the last key the last value. E.g. ilya's lid `[[133.36,112°],[133.88,1.42,outCubic],...]`.
- `springStep(t, freq=4, damping=0.35)`: unit-step response of a damped spring, `1 - exp(-ζ ω t) cos(ω √(1-ζ²) t)` with `ω = 2π·freq`, `t` in seconds since the event (0 for t<=0). Goes 0 → 1 with overshoot `exp(-πζ/√(1-ζ²))`: ζ 0.35 → 31%, 0.42 → 23%, 0.5 → 16%, 0.74 → 3%. First peak at `π/(ω√(1-ζ²))` (freq 3.2, ζ 0.42: 0.17 s). Used as `v += Δ * springStep(t - tKey, f, ζ)` for stepped values.
- `pulse(t, t0, halfLife=0.12)`: `0.5^((t-t0)/hl)` for t>=t0 else 0 — "hit" envelopes (kick pulses use hl 0.05–0.12).
- Deterministic randomness: `mulberry32(seed)`, `hash(...numbers)` (FNV-ish, [0,1)), `noise1/2/3`, `fbm1/2`; `frameIdx(t)`.
- Polylines: `polylineLengths(pts)`, `pointAtLength(pts, L, s)` → `{x,y,angle}` (reveals, text on path). `hexToLinear`, `vec2`, `dist`.

## E9. Driving the engine with a different song
Files to supply and their formats:
- **`data/audio.json`** (`AudioJSON`; loader tries `data/audio.json` then `data/audio.approx.json`; Vite serves the repo-root `data/` and `audio/` dirs):
  - `duration` (s), `bpm`, `beats[]` (seconds of every beat, ascending; the engine interpolates `beatAt/timeOfBeat` linearly between entries and extrapolates by the last period, so the array must cover the song), `downbeats[]` (seconds of every bar start), `sections[{name,start,end}]` (seconds; **timeline.ts requires a section named `outro`**, others are descriptive: intro, verse1, pre1, chorus1, break1, ... bridge, chorus4, outro), `fps` (envelope sample rate, 100 here), envelopes as top-level arrays (or nested under `features`) `rms, low, mid, high, vocal, drums, bass, other` — each `ceil(duration*fps)` floats in 0..1 (the original: 100 fps, frame i centred at i/100 s, 46 ms RMS window, 10 ms attack/90 ms release smoothing, each divided by its own 99th percentile and clipped; low < 150 Hz, mid 150–2000 Hz, high > 4 kHz of the mix; the last four are stem RMS), and `onsets{kick, snare, hat, vocal}` each `[[time_s, strength0..1], ...]` sorted by time. Extra fields (`beat_period, time_signature, notes`) are ignored.
  - Units: all times in seconds on the *gapless-decoded audio timeline* (the analysis shifted Demucs stems by -23 ms for LAME delay). The grid is a constant-tempo fit (`first beat 0.238 s`), bar phase resolved by harmony (chord change every 8 beats), sections start on downbeats. The analysis scripts are `analysis/analyze.py` (beats/downbeats/sections via librosa+scipy on Demucs stems) and `analysis/align.py` (word alignment).
- **`data/lyrics.json`** (`{lines:[{i, text, start, end, words:[{w, start, end, conf?, syl?:[[s,e],...]}]}], extras?, notes?}`): seconds; `text` and `w` use typewriter quotes in the data (the loader runs `smart()` for display and `plain()` is available); `syl` = per-syllable `[start,end]` windows (optional but used by `Lyrics.wordProgress` and dense-press `sungChars` for hyphenated words like "Post-Chinchilla,"); `conf` 0..1 alignment confidence; `extras[{start,end,desc}]` documents ad-libs. 46 lines, 227 words in the original. Words must be in time order with `start <= end` and gaps allowed (no overlap needed).
- **`audio/pdoom.mp3`** (path is hard-coded in `src/main.ts` and `scripts/render.ts`), `public/fonts/*.ttf` (+ `fonts/stroke/*.svg`; Archivo static width/weight instances produced by `analysis/make_fonts.py`), `public/plates/fig01..14.jpg` (outro's rewind stills; generate with `bun scripts/render.ts plates`, times in `app/plates.json`).
- **`src/timeline.ts`** must export `makeTimeline(ly: Lyrics, au: AudioData): TimelineEntry[]`. Pattern: helpers `cut(q, nth, tol)` = last beat at/before `ly.get(q, nth).words[0].start + tol` and `after(q)` = nearest downbeat to a line's end, then `E(id, sceneFile, start, end, {params, post, maxSamples})`; entries must tile the song without overlap (touching = hard cut) and the last `end` = `au.duration`. Every anchor string is a `ly.get()` substring match and **throws if absent**, so a new song needs a new anchor table; the `open` entry starts at 0. `outro` start comes from the `outro` section (falls back to `after('Was it all for show')`).
- **Things in the scenes that are bound to this song and need rewriting**, not just retiming: lyric strings via `lyrics.get('...')` in every scene (`stack`, `dense`, `loom`, `ilya`); `PDoom` (hud.ts) expects four `P(doom)` words and the fixed values 0.15/0.42/0.81/0.99; `outro` expects the section `outro` and ~36 beats of runway (`FADE0 = min(20+10, nBeats-6)`) and it re-instantiates the `open` scene and the 14-plate list/JPEGs; `loom` constructs `IlyaRoom` with `(ly, au, end, outroStart)` and reads the lines "foretold by Loom", "masked pre-training", "recursive self-upgrade", "What did Ilya", "all for show"; `dense` assumes line order "Post-Chinchilla / safety fence / Hundred thousand / RLHF" and 4 beats-per-line grids. Scenes are otherwise generic: they read only `ctx.audio/lyrics`, `f.t`, `f.a` and beat arrays, so a new song works with a new `timeline.ts` + new scenes that use the same helpers.
- **Beat-grid requirements the code relies on:** constant (or slowly varying) tempo with a beats array good to a few ms; downbeats array with 4 beats per bar (`barAt`); cut helpers snap to beats, so a 20–30 ms grid error shows as visibly late cuts.

## E10. Known engine quirks worth remembering
- `render.ts` sheets take ~2 minutes for 16 frames on CPU/SwiftShader; scene init (font load, shader compile, atlas building) dominates; a `404` browser log about a missing resource is harmless.
- `Layer2D.canvas.width` and `getImageData` are physical px (SCALE x); `drawImage(layer.canvas, x, y)` needs an explicit size.
- GLSL `fwidth`/`gl_FragCoord` are physical; use `FRAG_PX`, `PX_SCALE`, `pxLine`, `rampLine` for hairlines that keep their 1x ink at 4K.
- A depth-testing scene (stack's 3D line batch, `depthTest:true`) forces its sub-frames to run unmasked in adaptive sampling (costs full price); fine for a 6-s plate.
- Scenes may load assets (outro's JPEGs) in `init()`; a failed load yields `null` textures with a drawn placeholder rather than throwing.
