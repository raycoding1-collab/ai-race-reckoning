    A deliberately simple model, not a forecast. Labs grow capability by compounding. Safety spending slows growth and cuts the risk each lab adds. Risk grows with how capable a system is and how little safety it has. Catastrophe is drawn at random from the total risk, so every seed plays out differently. Rival labs are fictional and follow fixed strategies: Helix always races, Meridian starts cautious but cuts safety when it falls behind, and Vanta copies you. Share a race with ?seed=1234.
