元号

The Names of Time

Two hundred and forty-eight era names, and the year you were born in one of them.

Japanese documents are still dated by era: this year is Reiwa 8. The custom came from China with the Taika era in 645. In all there have been {{era_count}} era names, and since 1868 there has been one to each reign.{{fn:gengo}} Before then a new name could be proclaimed for an omen, a disaster or the start of a new cycle of the zodiac, and some lasted only months. For most of the seventh century there were none at all, and between 1331 and 1392 the rival Northern and Southern Courts each counted years in their own.{{fn:nanbokucho}}
