Return

Each thing goes back to its root.

A hanging scroll is read from the top down: sky and peaks first, then mist, then water and the small lives at the bottom. This page came down the same way, and now, as chapter 40 says, it turns back. The painting behind these words is the one you started with.

The paintings

The eight ink paintings behind this page were made for it, stroke by stroke, by a small program written for the purpose. Each exists in a day version, ink on paper, and a night version by moonlight; the taijitu button at the top of the page switches between them.
