流

Downstream

How the Way flowed into painting and poetry, and west along the old road.

Mountains and water

In Chinese, landscape painting is shanshui, mountains and water. Zong Bing (375–443) wrote the earliest surviving essay on it, and saw in the painted landscape a way to the Dao.{{fn:zong-bing}} Its most quoted line says of mountains and rivers 質有而趣靈: their substance is real, and they reach toward the spirit. A century later Xie He put first among the principles of painting qiyun shengdong, “spirit resonance, life’s motion”, the living qi a picture must carry.{{fn:chinese-painting}} Guo Xi’s Early Spring of 1072, now in the National Palace Museum in Taipei, is the great example of the Northern Song mountain style, and the essay attributed to him teaches the three distances: looking up at a peak, looking deep behind it, and looking level across to far hills.{{fn:guo-xi}}

Painters left much of the silk or paper bare, and the emptiness became mist, water and sky. The paintings on this site keep the same rule: every patch of mist is untouched paper.

Poets

Li Bai (701–762), perhaps the most loved of Chinese poets, went through life with a nickname given him by the older poet He Zhizhang: the Banished Immortal, an immortal exiled from heaven for a spell among mortals.{{fn:li-bai}} Retreat to the mountains, wine, moonlight and freedom from office are constant themes of Chinese poetry, and Taoist ones.

West along the old road

Laozi rode west and vanished. His book followed, much later.

  1. Early 18th century

    Jesuit missionaries in China make a complete Latin translation, the earliest into any foreign language. It stayed in manuscript until a Nankai University scholar published a transcription in 2026.{{fn:jesuit-latin}}

  2. 1842

    Stanislas Julien publishes the first complete translation in a Western language, in French.{{fn:julien}}

  3. 1868

    John Chalmers, a Scottish missionary, publishes the first English translation.{{fn:chalmers}}

  4. 1884

    Leo Tolstoy writes in his diary: “Having been translating Lao Tzu. Does not come out as I want.”{{fn:tolstoy}}

  5. 1929

    Richard Wilhelm and Carl Jung publish The Secret of the Golden Flower, a Taoist manual of inner alchemy, with Jung’s commentary.{{fn:golden-flower}}

  6. 1938

    In exile in Denmark, Bertolt Brecht writes a ballad of Laozi at the border: a customs man stops the old man’s ox and asks him to write down what he taught, that soft water in time wears away hard stone.{{fn:brecht}}

  7. 1946

    Martin Heidegger spends a summer in Freiburg translating parts of the Laozi into German with the Chinese scholar Paul Shih-yi Hsiao.{{fn:heidegger}}

  8. 1968

    The Beatles release George Harrison’s “The Inner Light”, a setting of chapter 47 in Juan Mascaró’s translation.{{fn:inner-light}} Read chapter 47

  9. 1988

    Ronald Reagan quotes chapter 60 to the United States Congress.{{fn:reagan}}

  10. 2022

    Misha Tadd’s bibliography counts 2,052 translations of the Laozi in 97 languages.{{fn:tadd}}
